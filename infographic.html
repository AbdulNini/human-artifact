AI Did Not Appear Out of Nowhere.

It is another wave in a much longer history of technological development.

Humans created tools to extend physical abilities, developed ways to store and reproduce information, built systems to communicate across distances, created machines that could process information, and then developed systems that could learn from data.

Each generation inherited technologies and knowledge from earlier generations and used them to create new possibilities.

The story does not necessarily end with AI. AI can become part of the next generation of technologies just as earlier technologies became part of the systems that came before it.
